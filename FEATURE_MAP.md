# Feature coverage map

This document shows where each selector group appears in the Green Coffee OS demo. The selector remains the source of truth as the client scope evolves.

| Source group | SaaS surface | Included capabilities |
|---|---|---|
| Website & brand | Platform setup → Website; Customer view | Responsive public experience, brand story, gallery controls, hours, map/contact, social links, light/dark appearance, accessibility |
| Digital menu & QR | Menu & QR; Customer view | 53 real café entries, exact DT prices, English/French switching, QR access, featured items, allergen tags and product-appropriate customization |
| Games, kids park & events | Games & events | Games library, 5 DT per-child Kids Park access, capacity check-in, football calendar, event capacity and WhatsApp/push match alerts |
| Reservations & notifications | Reservations | Request form/inbox, owner alerts, approval/refusal, confirmations and guest messages |
| Basic admin menu manager | Menu & QR; Overview | Owner workspace, add/edit/delete UI, image controls, price/stock/availability, overview quick actions |
| Customer accounts | Customers & loyalty; Customer view | Profiles, preferences, favourites, visit/order/reservation history and account-oriented customer preview |
| Advanced reservations | Reservations | Calendar, approval workflow, modification controls, reminders, waitlist and event reservations |
| Table QR ordering | Floor plan; Live orders; Guest/customer view | Scan-started 45-minute session, device verification, staff acceptance, table clearing/revocation, in-seat ordering and order notes |
| Staff dashboard & operations | Live orders; Floor plan | Interactive ticket board, KDS mode, accurate eight-table/three-PC-table venue map, kitchen/service/Kids Park zones, session lifecycle, shift notes and order-ready workflow |
| Payments & receipts | Platform setup → Payments; Orders | Konnect/Flouci/Paymee cards, payment states, online/cashier flows and digital receipt controls |
| Loyalty & promotions | Customers & loyalty; Campaigns | Points, coupons, happy-hour-ready campaigns, receipt QR sync, wallet, birthdays and Bronze/Silver/Gold/VIP tiers |
| Analytics & reports | Insights | Sales and product reports, reservation KPIs, peak-hour heatmap, QR scans, exports, behaviour insights and AI review digest |
| Marketing & feedback | Campaigns & feedback | Ratings, table feedback QR, WhatsApp/email broadcasts, CRM segments, push notifications and referrals |
| AI & automation | AI & automation; Menu & QR | AI menu copy/translations, café chatbot, product bundles, stock alerts and automated confirmation/promo/follow-up rules |
| Security, roles & back office | Platform setup → Team & security | Owner/manager/barista permission demo plus clearly labelled plans for production authentication, abuse controls, audit and backups |
| Launch & service | Platform setup → All modules | Successful front-end build plus launch, backend, deployment, maintenance and support requirements |

## Important scope note

The interfaces and front-end workflows are implemented for demonstration. Provider-backed functionality—including enforceable table sessions, payments, messaging, secure authentication, persistent cloud data, production AI, and real-time multi-device synchronization—needs backend implementation and credentials.
