# Generated menu image assets

The client-provided Green Coffee Games logo is copied from the feature selector to `public/logo.jpg` and is used in the login, staff shell, customer view, settings preview, favicon, and page metadata.

Six original menu photographs were generated with the built-in image generation tool and saved as optimized 900×900 WebP assets under `public/menu/`.

## Final prompt set

All six prompts used this shared production specification:

```text
Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: square coffee-shop digital menu product photo
Scene/backdrop: warm off-white limewash café surface with a softly blurred deep forest-green background
Style/medium: high-end photorealistic editorial food and beverage photography
Composition/framing: centered three-quarter view from slightly above, entire product visible, clean margins for menu-card cropping
Lighting/mood: soft window light from upper left, gentle realistic contact shadow, warm premium neighborhood café mood
Color palette: natural product colors, cream, deep forest green and restrained caramel accents
Constraints: no hands, people, text, logo, label or watermark; product in sharp focus
Avoid: illustration, CGI look, floating ingredients, excessive props, oversaturation
```

Subject-specific requests were:

1. A pale pistachio latte in a low clear glass with silky cold foam and crushed pistachios.
2. A tall layered espresso-and-milk iced latte with clear ice and a restrained salted-caramel ribbon.
3. An amber Ethiopian V60 service with handmade ceramic cup, glass dripper/server and roasted beans.
4. A clear tiramisu jar with espresso-soaked sponge, mascarpone layers, cocoa and one chocolate curl.
5. A naturally layered strawberry-pink, oat-milk and ceremonial-matcha iced latte with one strawberry garnish.
6. One richly golden all-butter croissant with crisp laminated layers and a few authentic flakes.

The generated PNG sources were converted to WebP for the application, reducing the combined asset payload from roughly 12.5 MB to under 550 KB.
